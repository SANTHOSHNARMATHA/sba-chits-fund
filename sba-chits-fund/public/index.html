How a Chit Fund Works

Four simple steps from enrollment to payout.

  1. 1

    Join a group

    You enroll in a chit group with a fixed value (eg. ₹ 5,00,000), duration (eg. 25 months) and a fixed number of subscribers (typically equal to the duration).

  2. 2

    Contribute monthly

    Each member contributes an equal monthly instalment. The total of all contributions in a month forms that month's prize pool.

  3. 3

    Open auction

    Each month, members can bid for the prize pool. The lowest bid wins - the discount becomes a dividend shared equally amongst all other subscribers.

  4. 4

    Receive your turn

    Every member receives the chit amount exactly once during the scheme. Until then you earn dividends; afterwards your future instalments continue uninterrupted.
